# Badge Social Sharing Guide

## Share Your Environmental Impact! 🌍

Your badges represent real environmental action. Sharing them on social media inspires others to join the movement and amplifies your impact. This guide will help you share your badges effectively across all platforms.

## Why Share Your Badges?

### Inspire Others
- Show friends what's possible
- Motivate others to take action
- Build environmental awareness
- Create positive peer pressure

### Build Your Brand
- Demonstrate environmental commitment
- Enhance professional reputation
- Connect with like-minded people
- Establish thought leadership

### Amplify Impact
- Spread the #GangGreen mission
- Recruit new platform members
- Increase environmental action
- Create viral moments

## Platform-Specific Guides

### Twitter/X 🐦

#### Best Practices

**Optimal Format:**
- Image size: 1200x675 pixels (landscape) or 1080x1080 (square)
- File format: PNG for best quality
- Character limit: 280 characters

**Posting Tips:**
1. Download badge as PNG (1024x1024)
2. Add compelling text
3. Include relevant hashtags
4. Tag @ganggreen
5. Post during peak hours (8-10 AM, 5-7 PM)

**Achievement Milestone:**

## Visual Best Practices

### Image Optimization

**For Social Media:**
1. Download badge as PNG (1024x1024)
2. Use high resolution
3. Ensure good lighting (if adding to photo)
4. Maintain aspect ratios
5. Compress for faster loading

**Design Tips:**
- Use clean backgrounds
- Ensure badge is prominent
- Add text overlays sparingly
- Maintain brand colors
- Keep it simple

### Creating Collages

**Badge Collection Showcase:**
1. Use Canva or similar tool
2. Create grid layout
3. Add all your badges
4. Include tier progression
5. Add text: "My #GangGreen Journey"

**Before/After:**
1. Split screen design
2. Left: Starting badges
3. Right: Current badges
4. Show growth over time
