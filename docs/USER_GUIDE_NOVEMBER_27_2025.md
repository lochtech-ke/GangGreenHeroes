## Getting Started

### What You'll Need

- **Email address** for account creation
- **Web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- **Internet connection**
- **Optional**: MetaMask wallet for Web3 features

### Quick Start (5 Minutes)

1. Visit [https://gg.lochtech.africa](https://gg.lochtech.africa)
2. Click "Get Started" or "Register"
3. Choose your account type
4. Fill in your details
5. Verify your email
6. Start your environmental journey!

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Registration

1. Click **"Register"** on the homepage
2. Choose your **account type** (Organization, Community, or Individual)
3. Select your **pilot forest**:
   - 🌳 **Kakamega Forest** - Kenya's last tropical rainforest
   - 🏙️ **Karura Forest** - Urban conservation area in Nairobi
   - 💧 **Mau Forest** - Critical water tower ecosystem

### Step 2: Fill Your Details

**All Users**:
- Full name
- Email address
- Password (minimum 8 characters)
- Confirm password

**Organizations Only**:
- Organization name
- Registration number
- Contact person
- Phone number

**Communities Only**:
- Community name
- Location
- Number of members

### Step 3: Verify Email

1. Check your email inbox
2. Click the verification link
3. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Step 4: Complete Profile

1. Upload profile picture (optional)
2. Add bio/description
3. Set notification preferences
4. Connect Web3 wallet (optional)

## Tree Planting Initiatives

### For Organizations: Creating Initiatives

#### Step 1: Navigate to Initiatives

Click **"Initiatives"** → **"Create New Initiative"**

#### Step 2: Basic Information

- **Initiative Name**: Descriptive title
- **Description**: Goals and objectives
- **Target Trees**: Number to plant
- **Duration**: Start and end dates
- **Forest Location**: Select pilot forest

#### Step 3: Location Details

Use the interactive map to:
- Pin exact location
- Draw planting area boundary
- Add multiple sites
- Set GPS coordinates

#### Step 4: Requirements

- **Volunteers Needed**: Number of participants
- **Budget**: Estimated costs
- **Resources**: Equipment, seedlings, etc.
- **Skills**: Required expertise

#### Step 5: Publish

- Review all details
- Click **"Publish Initiative"**
- Share with community

### For All Users: Joining Initiatives

#### Browse Initiatives

1. Go to **"Initiatives"** page
2. Filter by:
   - Forest location
   - Status (Active, Upcoming, Completed)
   - Type (Planting, Monitoring, Maintenance)
   - Distance from you

#### Join an Initiative

1. Click on initiative card
2. Read full details
3. Click **"Join Initiative"**
4. Confirm participation
5. Receive confirmation email

#### Track Your Participation

- View upcoming events
- Log your activities
- Upload photos
- Earn GG Coins
- Unlock achievements

## Tree Registry

### Registering a Tree

#### For Organizations

1. Go to **"Trees"** → **"Register New Tree"**
2. Fill in details:
   - Tree species
   - Planting date
   - Location (GPS)
   - Initiative (if applicable)
   - Planter name
3. Upload photo
4. Click **"Register Tree"**

#### Tree Information

Each tree record includes:
- Unique ID
- Species name (local and scientific)
- Age
- Location on map
- Health status
- Growth history
- Photos timeline

### Monitoring Trees

#### AI-Powered Health Analysis

Our Antugrow AI integration provides:
- **Health Score**: 0-100 rating
- **Growth Rate**: Compared to species average
- **Disease Detection**: Early warning system
- **Recommendations**: Care instructions

#### Update Tree Status

1. Visit tree profile
2. Click **"Update Status"**
3. Upload new photo
4. Add notes
5. AI analyzes automatically

#### View Tree History

- Growth timeline
- Health reports
- All photos
- Maintenance logs
- Carbon sequestration

NFT badges are digital achievements that:

- Are unique and verifiable
- Increase in value with rarity
- Unlock platform benefits

- **Benefit**: Biodiversity grants

- **How to Earn**: Complete major milestones

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is #GangGreen free to use?**  
A: Yes! Registration and basic features are completely free. You can earn GG Coins through activities to unlock premium features.

**Q: Which forests can I support?**  
A: Currently, we pilot in three Kenyan forests: Kakamega, Karura, and Mau. More forests coming soon!

**Q: Can I change my account type?**  
A: Yes, contact support to upgrade from Individual to Community or Organization.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and never share your data without permission.

### Tree Planting

**Q: Do I need to physically plant trees?**  
A: It depends on your account type. Organizations and Communities organize physical planting. Individuals can support virtually or join events.

**Q: How is tree health monitored?**  
A: We use Antugrow AI to analyze tree photos and provide health reports automatically.

**Q: Can I plant trees in my own area?**  
A: Currently, we focus on pilot forests. Contact us about expanding to your region.

### Badges & Coins

**Q: Can I sell my NFT badges?**  
A: Not yet, but we're working on a secondary marketplace.

**Q: Do GG Coins expire?**  
A: No, your coins never expire.

**Q: Can I transfer coins to friends?**  
A: Not currently, but this feature is planned.

**Q: What's the difference between traditional and geometric badges?**  
A: Just the visual style! Both have the same value and benefits. Choose based on your aesthetic preference.

### Payments

**Q: Are donations tax-deductible?**  
A: Depends on your country. We provide receipts for eligible donations.

**Q: What currencies do you accept?**  
A: Kenyan Shillings (KES) via Paystack. Cryptocurrency coming soon.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Yes, we use Paystack's PCI-compliant payment processing. We never store your card details.
