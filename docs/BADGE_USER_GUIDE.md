## Welcome to the New Geometric Badge System! 🎨

We're excited to introduce our beautiful new geometric badge designs! This guide will help you understand the new badge system, how to earn badges, and how to share your achievements.

## Viewing Your Badges

### On Your Profile

1. Go to your profile page
2. Click on the "Badges" tab
3. See all your earned badges in a beautiful grid
4. Click any badge to see details

### Badge Details

When you click a badge, you'll see:
- Badge name and tier
- Achievement type
- Date earned
- Number of times earned
- Forest where earned (if applicable)

### Badge Collection

Your badge collection shows:
- Total badges earned
- Progress toward next tier
- Rarest badges
- Recent achievements

## Sharing Your Badges

### Social Media Sharing

Share your achievements with friends and inspire others!

#### Twitter/X
1. Click the badge you want to share
2. Click "Share on Twitter"
3. Add your own message
4. Post!

**Example**: "I just earned my Gold Tree Planter badge on #GangGreen! 🌳 Join me in making Africa carbon-negative!"

#### Facebook
1. Click the badge
2. Click "Share on Facebook"
3. Add a caption
4. Share with your network

#### Instagram
1. Click the badge
2. Click "Download Image"
3. Open Instagram
4. Upload the badge image
5. Tag @ganggreen and use #GangGreenBadges

#### LinkedIn
1. Click the badge
2. Click "Share on LinkedIn"
3. Write about your environmental impact
4. Share with your professional network

### Download Options

You can download your badges in two formats:

**SVG (Vector)**
- Perfect for: Websites, presentations, printing
- File size: Very small (1-3KB)
- Quality: Scales to any size without losing quality

**PNG (Image)**
- Perfect for: Social media, profile pictures
- File size: Larger (50-200KB)
- Quality: High resolution (1024x1024 pixels)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happened to my old badges?

Your old badges have been automatically upgraded to the new geometric design. All your achievements are preserved - they just look better now!

### Can I keep the old design?

The new geometric design is now the standard for all badges. We believe you'll love the new look!

### Do I need to do anything?

No! The upgrade happened automatically. Just enjoy your beautiful new badges.

### Will this affect my achievement count?

No. All your achievements, dates, and progress are exactly the same. Only the visual design has changed.

### Can I customize my badge colors?

Currently, each achievement type has its own color palette. Custom colors may be available in the future!

### How do I earn the Hero badge?

The Hero badge can be earned by:
1. Purchasing it in the marketplace
2. Completing 500+ environmental actions
3. Winning special community challenges

### My badge isn't loading. What should I do?

Try these steps:
1. Refresh the page
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Check your internet connection
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### Can I print my badges?

Yes! Download the SVG version for the best print quality. The vector format ensures your badge looks crisp at any size.

### Are badges NFTs?

Yes! All badges are minted as NFTs on the blockchain, giving you true ownership of your achievements.

### Can I trade or sell my badges?

Hero badges and special event badges can be traded in the marketplace. Achievement badges are soulbound (non-transferable) to preserve their meaning.
