# Quick Start Guide

**Get started with #GangGreen in 5 minutes**

Welcome to #GangGreen! This guide will help you create your account, complete your first challenge, and start earning rewards.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account (2 minutes)

### Visit the Platform
Go to [https://gg.lochtech.africa](https://gg.lochtech.africa)

### Choose Sign Up Method

#### Option A: Email & Password (Recommended)
1. Click **"Sign Up"** in the top right
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a strong password
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email for verification link
6. Click the link to verify your account

#### Option B: Web3 Wallet
1. Click **"Connect Wallet"**
2. Choose MetaMask or WalletConnect
3. Approve the connection
4. Sign the verification message
5. Complete your profile

### Complete Your Profile
After signing up, you'll be guided to complete your profile:

1. **Full Name**: Your display name
2. **Role**: Choose your user type
   - Individual (most common)
   - Organization
   - Community Member
3. **Forest Preference**: Select your pilot forest
   - Kakamega Forest
   - Karura Forest
   - Mau Forest
4. **Optional**: Add phone, organization, location

## Step 2: Explore the Dashboard (1 minute)

After logging in, you'll see your personal dashboard with:

### Quick Stats
- **GG Coins Balance**: Your unified token balance (decimal precision up to 3 places)
- **Badges Earned**: Your achievement count
- **Actions Completed**: Your total impact
- **Current Rank**: Your leaderboard position

### Today's Challenges
Browse available micro-challenges:
- Plant a tree
- Share climate knowledge
- Join an initiative
- Complete a quiz
- Share on social media

### Recent Activity
See what's happening in the community:
- New initiatives
- Recent achievements
- Community posts
- Milestone celebrations

---

## Step 3: Complete Your First Challenge (2 minutes)

### Choose a Challenge
1. Browse the **"Today's Challenges"** section
2. Pick one that interests you
3. Click **"Start Challenge"**

### Popular First Challenges

#### 🌱 Plant Your First Tree
**Reward**: 10 GG Coins
1. Find a suitable location
2. Plant a tree (or join a planting event)
3. Take a photo
4. Upload to the platform
5. Add location and species
6. Submit for verification

#### 📚 Learn About Climate Action
**Reward**: 5 GG Coins
1. Click "Start Challenge"
2. Read the educational content
3. Complete the short quiz
4. Submit your answers
5. Earn your reward instantly

#### 🤝 Join an Initiative
**Reward**: 5 GG Coins
1. Browse active initiatives
2. Find one in your area
3. Click "Join Initiative"
4. Confirm your participation
5. Earn your reward

#### 📱 Share on Social Media
**Reward**: 2 GG Coins
1. Create a post about climate action
2. Share on your social media
3. Copy the post link
4. Submit to the platform
5. Earn your reward

### Submit Your Challenge
1. Follow the challenge instructions
2. Provide required proof (photo, link, etc.)
3. Click **"Submit Challenge"**
4. Wait for verification (usually instant)
5. Receive your GG Coins!

---

## Step 4: Earn Your First Badge (Bonus)

### Bronze Badge Requirements
Complete **10 actions** to unlock your first badge:
- Plant 2 trees (20 GG Coins)
- Join 1 initiative (5 GG Coins)
- Complete 5 micro-challenges (15-25 GG Coins)
- Share 2 social posts (4 GG Coins)

### Track Your Progress
Check your badge progress in:
- Dashboard → Badges section
- Profile → Achievements tab
- Navigation → Badge icon

### Unlock Your Badge
When you reach 10 actions:
1. You'll receive a notification
2. Visit the Marketplace
3. Purchase your Bronze Badge (50 GG Coins)
4. Badge appears in your profile
5. Share your achievement!

## Common First-Time Questions

### How do I earn GG Coins?
Complete any action on the platform:
- Tree planting (50 GG Coins base)
- Mission completion (100 GG Coins base)
- Learning modules (20 GG Coins base)
- Community posts (5 GG Coins base)
- Petition signatures (10 GG Coins base)
- Referrals (50 GG Coins base)
- Daily login (5 GG Coins base)

Rewards support multipliers for verified actions, streaks, and badge tiers!

### What can I do with GG Coins?
- Purchase NFT badges
- Unlock premium features (coming soon)
- Trade with others (coming soon)
- Donate to initiatives (coming soon)

### How are GG Coins stored?
GG Coins use decimal precision (up to 3 decimal places) allowing for micro-rewards and flexible pricing. Your balance is cached for fast access and updates in real-time when you earn or spend coins.

### How are my actions verified?
- **Instant**: Quiz completions, social shares
- **AI-Powered**: Tree planting (Antugrow API)
- **Blockchain**: All transactions recorded
- **Community**: Peer verification for some actions

### Is my data secure?
Yes! We use:
- End-to-end encryption
- Secure authentication
- Privacy-first design
- GDPR compliance
- Regular security audits

### Can I use this on mobile?
Absolutely! The platform is fully responsive:
- Works on any device
- Mobile-optimized interface
- Bottom navigation for easy access
- Touch-friendly interactions
