## Hero and Background Images

### Kenyan Forest Landscapes
High-quality forest photography showcasing Kenya's diverse ecosystems.

**Kakamega Forest:**
- Primary tropical rainforest in Western Kenya
- Rich biodiversity and indigenous tree species
- Images show dense canopy and forest floor

**Karura Forest:**
- Urban forest in Nairobi
- Community conservation success story
- Images show accessible trails and diverse wildlife

**Mau Forest:**
- Kenya's largest indigenous montane forest
- Critical water tower for the region
- Images show highland forest ecosystem

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Attribution Required:** Always credit The Green Belt Movement for community images
2. **Non-Commercial Use:** Images are used for conservation education and platform promotion
3. **Respect Privacy:** Community member faces are shown with consent
4. **Cultural Sensitivity:** Images represent authentic African conservation efforts
5. **Quality Standards:** All images optimized for web (WebP format with JPEG fallback)

## Image Optimization

All images have been:
- Compressed to < 100KB where possible
